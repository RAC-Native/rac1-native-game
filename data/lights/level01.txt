# Novalis. The sun raised from the key light's 33 degrees to 43 (Daino's choice in the sun editor; 55 was tried first), its azimuth
# turned to 0 (toward +x). At 33 degrees the rock walls and the plateau beside the start valley keep it in shade all day: along the
# first walk (the start, the landing pads, the river) a third of what faces the sun saw it; higher, most of it does and the walls'
# shadows fall at their feet. Our choice (the sun editor, docs/lighting.md "The level's own sun"); the light set's colour is kept.
