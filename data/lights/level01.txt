# aimed down (0.85: a pool of light on the floor below, the wall under the fixture washed, the ceiling and the fixture left dark);
# 5.0 and 13 units so the floor some 6 units below shows it against the painted light (1.3 and 11 every way alike barely did).
# #102: the light raised from 1.7 units under the panel to about 0.4 (z -17.648 -> -4.0): the pool sat low on the wall under the
